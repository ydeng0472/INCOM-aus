Material corrections made
1. Distinguish the EOQD response approximation from the finite-stock, buffered/rationed operational simulation. The existing responses do not establish an equilibrium of the coupled system.
2. Participation invariance does not imply unchanged follower profits or zero follower disruption costs.
3. rho>A is strict structural shortage; rho=A is the critical boundary. P>=D/A remains a necessary, not sufficient, condition for full nominal supply.
4. The buffer target minimises a surrogate objective. The regime switch is a proposed heuristic, not a demonstrated optimal switch.
5. B_cap=(P-D)/lambda is a mean potential refill volume over an up spell, not a sustainable-stock cap. At fixed D its limit as rho approaches A is D/mu, not zero. Mean supply slack AP-D is what vanishes.
6. Posterior means and maximum-likelihood estimates are distinct. Ordinary gamma conjugacy requires transition counts and corresponding exposure times; discounted tracking is a heuristic.
7. Restrict calibration, validation, insurance and learning claims to synthetic scenarios and tested policies.
8. Pin Brero et al. (2024) to arXiv:2210.03852v4 and replace selected industry claims with primary-source evidence.

AUTHOR CHECKS REQUIRED BEFORE SUBMISSION
A. Define the leader's objective and solution procedure, including premium-grid evaluation, buffer selection, and ties. Confirm whether the paper claims an approximate policy framework or a full coupled Stackelberg equilibrium; the latter requires follower best responses incorporating stock and rationing.
B. In the critical/short regime, does the target Z* represent total leader stock S or extra buffer B? S=B+max Q elsewhere permits two different implementations. Resolve from the original code and recheck all affected results.
C. When a follower does not participate, is D total potential market demand or active-buyer demand? State the convention for capacity ratios, replenishment calculations and fill denominators.
D. Can buffer stock reach buyers during a route closure? If not, a plant outage and route closure cannot share the same protective-stock mechanism without a location/delivery assumption. The revision flags this limitation rather than redesigning the model.
E. Confirm cumulative statistics, exact exposure/transition observations, gamma prior shapes/rates and prior strength. If the simulator observes only daily endpoints, the continuous-time gamma likelihood is not exact. For forgetting, specify how both counts and exposure are discounted.
F. Define each profit gap and adaptation-recovery percentage, its denominator, and the aggregation over seeds versus scenarios. Clarify evaluation years versus warm-up years and confirm that standard errors use independent seed-level paired differences. Shared seed labels alone do not guarantee common exogenous paths if policies consume random numbers differently.
G. Supply the complete Q-learning protocol (states, actions, reward, discount factor, learning and exploration schedules, training seeds, evaluation seeds, evaluation with/without exploration and training budget). The red wording restricts claims to the tested tabular implementation.
H. The six original source assets are now restored. numbers.tex reports 108 sensitivity cases and 6 of 12 strict-short regime/utilisation combinations, consistent with the algebraic design checks. Original simulation code and independent run outputs are still needed to validate the numerical claims. No new result values have been generated.
I. Complete the authors' AI-use declaration according to the eventual venue rules. The supplied placeholder has been preserved rather than replaced with invented author attestations.
J. Reapply the confirmed conference template and page limit only after selecting the submission route. The supplied A4 article class and author names were preserved; this review does not certify venue-format compliance.
